Welcome to the
Bowling Alley

New to bowling? No problem.

This sheet keeps score for you. Strikes, spares and bonus points are all added up automatically.

Bowling a game
  1. Tap + Add Bowlers and type everyone’s names.
  2. Tap Begin Bowling.
  3. Tap the first box to open the keypad.
  4. After each ball, tap how many pins fell. The sheet moves to the next bowler on its own.
When a game ends
  1. Tap Begin New Game to play another. Everyone’s scores so far are kept.
Done for the night
  1. Tap Finish Bowling.
  2. To keep your scores, tap Print or Export (saves a file or sends it from your phone).
  3. Tap Exit App to clear the sheet for the next group.
What the keypad buttons mean
1–9How many pins fell with that ball XStrike: all 10 pins down with the first ball /Spare: the rest of the pins down with the second ball -Miss: no pins fell (a gutter ball) FFoul: stepped over the line, so that ball scores 0 ⌫Undo the last box ← →Move to the box before or after DoneClose the keypad

Each bowler gets 2 balls per frame and 10 frames per game. A strike or spare earns bonus pins from the next ball or two, added in for you. A strike or spare in the 10th frame earns an extra ball.
